# Open challenges

*[Version française](DEFIS.md)*

Each challenge points to a part of the dossier (https://ares7-projet.netlify.app/dossier.html, in French; `lire.html` is the plain-text version, easy to machine-translate).
A challenge is met when someone shows, with a calculation or a source, that the point holds… or that it does not. Both answers move the project forward.

## 1. Power density 🔵 (the lock)
The link between transit time and on-board power is what blocks everything (dossier: *Les nombres* and *Le laboratoire*).
**Challenge:** find a source or an architecture that narrows the gap, or show that the gap is larger than the dossier says.

## 2. The 60/60 split 🟢
The dossier claims that the frequency-2 geodesic forces a split into two families of 60 edges (*L'invention — 60/60*).
**Challenge:** find a competing split that would do better, or a reason why this one would not work.

## 3. The magnetic shield 🔵 (contested)
The field required is small; the real problem is inflating the bubble by plasma injection (*Bouclier — SPE + GCR*).
**Challenge:** demonstrate or refute that inflation at vehicle scale.

## 4. The radiators
At full power, about 1.9 MW of heat must be rejected: the difficulty is the area to deploy (85 to 320 m²) around a 12 m sphere, not the mass (*Le laboratoire*).
**Challenge:** propose a credible deployable radiator for that area.

## 5. The crew ⛔ (for now)
There is room on board, but the radiation dose received on a mission exceeds the limits: ARÈS-7 stays a drone until the reactor delivers ×3 to ×5.5 more power (*Habitat & équipage*).
**Challenge:** find a shield or a trajectory that makes a crew possible.

## 6. The light of the edges 🔵
No halo: established (vacuum does not scatter light). Bright light: plausible. Colour: to be demonstrated (*Physique & lumière*).
**Challenge:** predict the actual colour from the physics of the plasma.
